The math, plainly.

Airbnb takes 15.5% of every booking, including the ones from guests who’ve already stayed with you once. A direct platform doesn’t need to replace your Airbnb listing to be worth building—it needs a handful of returning guests to book direct instead.

At a $1,000 nightly rate, the platform typically pays for itself within about six returning-guest bookings. Every booking after that is yours, without the fee.

More than one property?

The guest portal and platform architecture are built once and reused across every property in your portfolio—only the branding, copy, and details change. That means each additional property costs half what the first one does.

Two properties or three—if that’s your situation, the second and third platforms aren’t separate projects. They’re extensions of the first.
